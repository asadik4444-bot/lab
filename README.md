# The Lab

Large US companies, deal financing and M&A activity, built from public filings and statistics:
SEC EDGAR, the ECB, Eurostat, the US Treasury, the New York Fed and the Swiss National Bank.
Live at https://lab.aabdurashidov.ch/ (part of https://aabdurashidov.ch).

- **Static site:** these files are generated by a private data pipeline. Every figure links to its source on the
  Method page; data that is missing is shown as missing, never estimated.
- **Market prices are not shown:** the company pages take a share price that you type, and compute in your browser.
- **Previous version:** `v1/` is the earlier Lab, kept for comparison, on the same data.
- **Fonts:** Mona Sans and Instrument Serif, SIL Open Font License (`fonts/`).
- **Logos:** Simple Icons (CC0) and SVGL (MIT); see `assets/logos/`.

Hypothetical models on stated assumptions. Not investment advice. No price targets.
